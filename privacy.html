Padma Hostel Finance – Privacy Policy

Padma Hostel Finance is the record-keeping app of Padma Hostel, Lalitpur: residents, fees, dues, receipts, expenses and reports.

Your account

The admin makes a username and password for each person. Your name (as the admin entered it) is shown in the admin panel. No Google account is needed to use the app.

Where your records are kept

Your records are stored in the browser storage of your own computer, so the app works without internet. A copy (data backup and PDF reports) is uploaded to your own folder in Padma Hostel's Google Drive, which is managed by the admin.

A compressed copy of your records is also kept on the app's server so the admin panel can show it, even before Drive is connected.

The admin can view your records (read-only) for support and safe-keeping. Records are not sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

The admin can freeze or remove an account. The records on your own computer are never deleted by the admin.

Form scanner

When you choose to scan an old paper application form, its photo is sent to Google Gemini to read the text, then discarded. It is not stored by the app.

Student details

Admission forms contain students' personal details. They are used only to run the hostel and are kept as long as the hostel needs them. A student can ask the hostel to correct or remove their details.
