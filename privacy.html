Padma Hostel Finance – Privacy

Padma Hostel Finance is a private record-keeping app used by Padma Hostel, Lalitpur, Nepal, to manage its own residents, fees and expenses.

Where the data is kept

Records are stored in the browser storage of the hostel's own computers. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google Drive

If the hostel owner connects Google Drive, the app uses the drive.file permission. It saves backup files and PDF reports into a folder the app created in the owner's Google Drive, and it can only see files it created. It cannot open any other files in that Drive. The permission can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.

Form scanner

When staff choose to scan an old paper application form, its photo is sent to Google Gemini to read the text, then discarded by the app. It is not stored by the app.
