Common questions

How do I admit a new student?

Go to Admissions, fill in the form (same as the paper form), choose the room and bed, check the joining bill and press 💾 Save Admission. You can also scan an old paper form with 📷 Choose / take photo of form.

How do I give a discount?

In the Joining Bill Summary, press ✏ Edit, change the amounts and write a reason if you like. Press ✔ Done. The discount shows on the bill and the PDF.

How do I record a monthly fee?

Open Monthly Fee, choose the month, and click Receive payment next to the student. For next month, change the month at the top first.

Who owes money, and whose deposit must be returned?

Open Balance Sheet. Colour signs show who has paid, who owes fees, and which deposits to give back.

What does the Save button do?

💾 Save makes the monthly PDF reports (income, expenditure, fees, admission forms, profit or loss) and puts them with your data backup in Google Drive. Pressing it again only updates what changed.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Backups and the admin copy are sent automatically the next time you are online. Scanning forms needs internet.

"A new version is ready — Update now"

Click Update now. Your records are not touched by updates.

I forgot my password

Ask the hostel admin to set a new password for you from the admin panel.
